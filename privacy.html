This website is part of Team 6’s Digital Startup course project. We are exploring interest in a study and well-being companion, not providing a medical service.

The testing list

If you register, we store your email, optional programme or study year, your confirmation that you are an adult higher-education student, the time you consented and the recruitment channel. Team 6 uses this information to organise Cogniflow research and contact volunteers. Registration does not subscribe you to marketing or create a product account.

No health records or check-in answers are collected here. The prototype snapshots on the home page use sample data and collect no health information. We do not sell registration information or send it to external analytics or mailing services.

Registrations are held for up to 90 days. Expired records are removed when the server next processes a request. Only the team operating the server should have access to the registration database. If you make a withdrawal request, the active database record is deleted. A team member must also remove any separate export they made.

Optional visit counting

Visit counting is off unless you choose “I’m a student, count my visit”. That choice confirms you are 18 or over and in higher education. With your permission, a random ID is kept on this browser for 30 days. The server records that ID, timestamps, your recruitment channel and visit or call-to-action events. It can associate a completed registration with the anonymous ID to calculate conversion, but the event log does not contain your email.

You can browse and register without allowing this measurement. The settings link on the home page lets you turn off future counting. Anonymous event records expire after 30 days. Resetting your browser or using another device can create another ID. We do not store IP addresses in the application logs. A future hosting provider may maintain its own technical logs.

Remove your registration

Use the private withdrawal code shown when you first registered. The code is a key to deleting that registration, so keep it private. It is not emailed to you. If you registered before, a repeated submission will not reveal a new code.

For a classroom demonstration, use a test email. Before collecting real students’ details, the team should publish a monitored contact address here so people who lose their code can also reach the project organisers.
